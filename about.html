About

We tell your story, and we do it beautifully.

Our films draw the attention and support of those who are watching you, as they are inspired by your authenticity.

We capture your journey: the milestones, the ups and downs, wins and losses, your faith, your love, your truth, your creation, and everything in between.

We reveal it in one stunning tell-all film.

With YOU at the centre of it.

Founder and CEO

Rania Elewa

Rania founded Heartline Productions in Lagos in 2026 to make founder story films for founders and creators. She directs every film the company makes.

She works with people who want their story told in a truthful, beautiful way, and who value honesty and integrity in filmmaking. The film is finished when it is true, not when it is polished.

Values

What we believe

  • 01 Truth over polish Honesty and integrity in filmmaking come before a perfect frame.
  • 02 Being truly seen We film the milestones, the ups and downs, the wins and the losses, and the faith that carried you through them.
  • 03 A legacy piece One film your family, your team and your customers can watch for years.
