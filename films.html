Process

How a film is made

  • 01
    A conversation

    We talk through how the company started, who it serves, and what you want people to understand.

  • 02
    The shoot

    A small crew films the interview and the work as it happens, usually over one or two days.

  • 03
    The edit

    The footage is cut into one film, and you review a first version before anything is locked.

  • 04
    The film

    You receive the finished film in the formats you need, and it is published here as a case study.
