The story

A founder story film follows one person and the company they built. It starts with a long conversation about the decision to begin, the years before anyone was watching, and the reason the work still matters.

The shoot moves between that conversation and the business itself. We film the room, the hands, the products, and the people who show up every day, so the story is carried by the work and not only by the talking.

The finished film is short enough to watch in one sitting. It is made for the founder's own audience, and it is the film she sends when someone asks what she does.
